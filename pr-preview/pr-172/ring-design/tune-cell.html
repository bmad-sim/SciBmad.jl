7. Tune Cell¶

Introduction¶

In order to adjust the tunes of the ring, the phase advance per FODO cell in one of the straight sections can be adjusted, while ensuring that it is still matched to the periodic solution in each of the surrounding arcs. We’ll put this “tune cell” in the 2 o’clock straight section, between the sextants 1 and 3, and name its quadrupoles by appending _2 to the names of the quadrupoles they replace.

Required for the match is:

  1. Setting the phase advances in the fodossf_2/fodossr_2 cells of the straight section for the desired tunes \(Q_1 = 54.08\) and \(Q_2 = 54.14\) (2 constraints).

  2. Matching from the dispersion suppressor to the new periodic Twiss parameters of the fodossf_2/fodossr_2 cells (4 constraints).

  3. Matching the fodossf_2/fodossr_2 cells to the dispersion creator (4 more constraints, but these are already met by the mirror symmetry).

These 6 constraints can be met using the four quadrupoles of the forward matching section (qff2_2, qdf2_2, qff3_2, and qdf3_2) and the two quadrupoles of the straight section cells (qfss_2 and qdss_2).

  1. Define the new quadrupoles, starting with the strengths of those they replace:

@elements begin
  qfss_2 = Quadrupole(L=0.5, Kn1=qfss.Kn1)
  qdss_2 = Quadrupole(L=0.5, Kn1=qdss.Kn1)
  qff2_2 = Quadrupole(L=0.5, Kn1=qff2.Kn1)
  qdf2_2 = Quadrupole(L=0.5, Kn1=qdf2.Kn1)
  qff3_2 = Quadrupole(L=0.5, Kn1=qff3.Kn1)
  qdf3_2 = Quadrupole(L=0.5, Kn1=qdf3.Kn1)
end;

  1. Define the lines of the tune cell, and put them into the sextants 1 and 3. The dispersion suppressor and the dispersion creator keep their quadrupoles:

fodossf_2 = [qfss_2, d1, db, d2, qdss_2, d1, db, d2]
fodossr_2 = [qfss_2, d2, db, d1, qdss_2, d2, db, d1]
mssf_2 = [qff2_2, d1, db, d2, qdf2_2, d1, db, d2, qff3_2, d1, db, d2, qdf3_2, d1, db, d2]
mdcr_2 = [qfss_2, d2, db, d1, qdf3_2, d2, db, d1, qff3_2, d2, db, d1, qdf2_2, d2, db, d1]
dispcrer_2 = [qff2_2, d2, bh, d1, qdf1, d2, bh, d1, qff1, d2, bh, d1, qd, d2, bh, d1]
arc_to_ssf_2 = [dispsupf; mssf_2]
ss_to_arcr_2 = [mdcr_2; dispcrer_2]

sextant1 = [repeat(fodossf, 4); ss_to_arcf; repeat(fodoaf, 20); arc_to_ssf_2;
            repeat(fodossf_2, 4)]
sextant3 = [repeat(fodossr_2, 4); ss_to_arcr_2; repeat(fodoar, 20); arc_to_ssr;
            repeat(fodossr, 4)]

ring = Beamline([sextant1; sextant3; sextant5; sextant7; sextant9; sextant11],
                species_ref=electron, pc_ref=pc_ref)

  1. The targets need three TwissExprs: of the new straight section cell (its periodic Twiss parameters), of the line from the arc into the new straight section (the first half of the tune cell), and of the ring (its tunes):

cell_ss2 = Beamline(fodossf_2, species_ref=electron, pc_ref=pc_ref)
line_2 = Beamline(arc_to_ssf_2, species_ref=electron, pc_ref=pc_ref)

tw_ss2 = TwissExpr(cell_ss2)
tw_2 = TwissExpr(line_2, a_initial=tw_af.a[end])
tw_ring = TwissExpr(ring)

TwissExpr of a Beamline with 1707 elements

All three depend on the knobs, and match! computes each of them once per evaluation.

  1. Run the match:

match!(vary=[@knob(qfss_2.Kn1), @knob(qdss_2.Kn1), @knob(qff2_2.Kn1), @knob(qdf2_2.Kn1),
             @knob(qff3_2.Kn1), @knob(qdf3_2.Kn1)],
       targets=[tw_2.beta1[end] == tw_ss2.beta1[begin],
                tw_2.alpha1[end] == tw_ss2.alpha1[begin],
                tw_2.beta2[end] == tw_ss2.beta2[begin],
                tw_2.alpha2[end] == tw_ss2.alpha2[begin],
                tw_ring.q1 == 54.08, tw_ring.q2 == 54.14])

The tunes of the ring are now 54.08 and 54.14, and the beta functions are only changed in the tune cell:

tw = twiss(ring)
using CairoMakie
fig = Figure(size=(800, 300))
ax = Axis(fig[1, 1], xlabel="s [m]", ylabel="β [m]")
rows = (0.0, 1300.0) # The sextants 1 and 3
lines!(ax, tw.s[rows], tw.beta1[rows], label="β₁")
lines!(ax, tw.s[rows], tw.beta2[rows], label="β₂")
axislegend(ax, position=:rt)

Exercises¶

  1. Periodicity inside the tune cell: In the Bmad version of this tutorial, the periodicity of the Twiss parameters inside the tune cell was a target of its own: the Twiss parameters at the fourth qfss_2 of the ring must be equal to those at the fifth. Write these targets with tw_ring only. Hint: ring[[qfss_2]] is the vector of all placements of qfss_2 in the ring, and each of them is a location by itself.

  2. Other tunes: Change the tunes to 54.12 and 54.18. How much do the quadrupole strengths of the tune cell change? Which tunes can the tune cell reach?

The lattice file of this chapter is lattices/07-tune-cell.jl.
